About My Development

During this module I developed a range of employability skills by turning a transport problem into a structured venture idea. I worked with business planning tools such as the Business Model Canvas and Lean Canvas, considered customers and competitors, explored ethics and sustainability, and prepared material for a short investor pitch.

My project focused on making journeys around London simpler, more accessible and more sustainable. This required me to think creatively, research a problem, communicate a value proposition and make decisions about how a venture could work in practice.

Core Employability Skills

Communication

Explained the venture idea, value proposition and customer benefits clearly in written and presentation formats.

Problem-solving

Identified journey complexity, disruption and accessibility gaps and developed practical solutions.

Creativity & Innovation

Generated a new service concept and explored ways technology and transport partnerships could improve the customer experience.

Digital Skills

Used digital tools to create presentations, visual concepts, structured business models and this portfolio webpage.

Research & Analysis

Considered customers, alternatives, market needs, success measures and evidence needed to test the venture.

Entrepreneurial Thinking

Used Lean Canvas and Business Model Canvas thinking to connect customers, problems, solutions, costs, channels and revenue.

Sustainability & Ethics

Considered accessibility, privacy, inclusion, environmental impact and responsible partnerships.

Presentation & Time Management

Selected the strongest points for a concise five-minute investor pitch and organised work around assessment requirements.
